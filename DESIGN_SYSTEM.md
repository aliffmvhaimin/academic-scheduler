# Neo-Brutalist Design System

## Design Philosophy

The application uses neo-brutalist visual design.

The interface should feel:
- bold
- playful
- functional
- high contrast
- slightly raw
- highly readable

The design must prioritize usability over decoration.

---

## Borders

Default border:
3px solid #000000

Important controls:
4px solid #000000

---

## Shadows

Use hard offset shadows.

Default:
4px 4px 0px #000000

Large:
6px 6px 0px #000000

Never use:
- blurred shadows
- large soft shadows
- glass effects

---

## Border Radius

Default:
4px

Cards:
4px

Buttons:
4px

Avoid:
- pill-shaped controls
- excessive rounding

---

## Colour System

Primary background:
#F5F1E8

Primary text:
#000000

Primary accent:
#FFDE59

Secondary accent:
#FF6B6B

Success:
#7DFF7A

Information:
#6CCFF6

White:
#FFFFFF

Use colour primarily to communicate hierarchy,
status and interaction.

Do not use more than 2–3 accent colours on a single screen.

---

## Typography

Headings:
bold/heavy sans-serif

Body:
highly readable sans-serif

Important numbers:
extra-bold

Avoid:
thin typography

---

## Buttons

Buttons must:
- have black border
- have hard shadow
- have strong text
- provide visible pressed state

Pressed state:

transform:
4px right
4px down

shadow:
0px 0px 0px black

---

## Cards

Cards use:

background colour
+
3px black border
+
4px hard shadow

Cards should communicate information,
not simply decorate the screen.

---

## Icons

Use consistent outlined icons.

Do not mix multiple icon styles.

---

## Accessibility

Text must remain readable against its background.

Do not communicate status using colour alone.

Interactive elements must have obvious states.

Minimum touch target:
44 × 44 dp.

---

## Animation

Animations should be short and functional.

Use animation for:
- navigation
- schedule generation
- task completion
- button feedback

Avoid:
- excessive bouncing
- decorative animation
- long transitions

---

## Responsive Design

The primary target is mobile.

Design for:
- small Android phones
- standard Android phones
- larger iPhones

Avoid fixed-width layouts.
